# Privacy Policy for Paawmodoro

**Last updated: 6 August 2026**

## The short version

Paawmodoro does not collect any data about you. None. It has no analytics, no
advertising, no accounts, no tracking, and no servers of its own.

The app contains **no networking code at all**. It cannot send data anywhere,
because there is nothing in it that talks to a network. It works in aeroplane
mode, permanently.

## What is stored, and where

Everything Paawmodoro remembers stays on your device, in the app's own private
storage:

- Your completed focus sessions, and the dates and lengths of them
- Your settings — chosen companion, place, theme, sounds, durations
- What you have found: field journal sightings, constellations, dreams,
  postcards, and your bond level
- Names you have given your companions

This information never leaves your iPhone. It is not backed up to any service
run by the developer. If your device is included in your own iCloud or computer
backups, this data is included in those backups under your control, subject to
Apple's terms — the developer has no access to them.

Deleting the app deletes all of it, permanently and irreversibly.

## Purchases

Paawmodoro offers optional in-app purchases (a one-time "Paawmodoro Plus"
unlock and three optional tips). These are processed entirely by Apple through
the App Store.

The developer never sees, receives, or stores your payment details, card
numbers, billing address, or Apple Account information. The app only ever
learns whether a purchase was completed, which Apple tells it. Apple's handling
of that transaction is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, Paawmodoro schedules them locally on your device to
tell you when a focus phase or break has ended. These are created and delivered
by iOS on the device itself. No push server is involved, and no notification
content is transmitted anywhere.

You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Notifications.

## Data the developer receives

None.

The developer receives no personal data, no usage data, no diagnostics, no
identifiers, and no crash reports from this app. Apple may provide the
developer with anonymous, aggregated sales and download statistics through App
Store Connect; these contain no information that identifies any individual
person or device.

## Children

Paawmodoro is rated 4+ and is safe for children. Because the app collects no
data whatsoever, it collects no data from children either. There are no
accounts, no chat, no user-generated content, no links out to the web, and no
advertising.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this address
and the "last updated" date above will change. Since the app collects nothing,
any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a change in practice.
